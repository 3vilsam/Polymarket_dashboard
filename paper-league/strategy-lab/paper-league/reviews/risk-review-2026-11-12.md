## Common perp engine (n1–n7), checked in code
- Sizing (league.py `open_paper`): budget = min(0.5%·eq, 2%·eq − open risk), and qty = floor_sz(budget / cost-inclusive risk per unit). Costs are 2bp entry slip + 0.045% taker in + 0.045% taker out + 5bp stop slip + a 24h funding reserve of at least 0.00125%/h, about 0.19% in total, which matches the desk stress. Notional is capped at 3× eq. A book with notional under 10 USD rejects the trade. Sizes are floored to szDecimals from live meta. Stops must be 0.5%–4.5% at entry, otherwise the book abstains. Each book holds one position at most (cluster_max=1). The −2% SOD halt is sticky. Hourly funding is charged, and `enforce_cost_risk` closes a position whose funding uses up its budget.
- Min notional at 100 eq: 0.50/(stop+0.19%) gives 72.0 USD at a 0.5% stop, 10.66 at 4.5% and 11.93 at 4.0%, so all are ≥10 (OK). The highest effective leverage is ~0.72× at the 0.5% stop (OK ≤3×). At a 4.5% stop, adverse funding above ~0.0067%/h pushes notional under 10, and the engine rejects the trade. That is the correct behaviour.
- Stops are virtual 1m-candle replays (touch = trigger+5bp, gap = open+5bp), not exchange orders. That is acceptable for paper. Hyperliquid perps support native trigger stops, so the stop logic can carry over to a ticket.

## Common spot engine (sp4), checked in code
- `spot_open4`: qty = floor_sz(eq/(fill·(1+0.07%))), i.e. **ALL-IN CASH (~16.2 USD notional at 1×)**. There is **no risk-fraction sizing**. Fees are 0.07% spot taker each side, slippage is max(2bp, L2 walk) with 5bp on stops, the 10 USD minimum applies and the −2% sticky halt applies. Stops are virtual replays. **Hyperliquid spot exchange trigger stops are still UNVERIFIED on testnet.**
- Loss at the stop is ≈ notional·(stop+~0.21%), so a 0.5% stop loses 0.115 USDC (0.71% eq) and a 4.5% stop loses 0.765 USDC (4.71% eq). That is over v1's 0.5% cap at every allowed stop width. Any stop wider than ~1.8% also trips v1's −2% daily halt in a single trade.
- At v1's 0.5% (0.081 USDC risk), notional = 0.081/(stop+0.19%) reaches 10 USD only if the stop is ≤0.62%.
- The sp4 books do **not** cite the draft. They take the owner-reported 16.24 equity (unverified) and the v3 spot all-in convention. Their sizing fits neither v1 nor the draft: the draft has a 10–12 USD max order and 3% risk, and all-in exceeds 3% once the stop is wider than ~2.8%.
