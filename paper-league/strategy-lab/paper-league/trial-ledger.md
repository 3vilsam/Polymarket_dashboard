# Trial ledger note

Scale cohort `-k1000`: one twin per active book, 1,000 virtual USDT bankroll, identical frozen rules and risk (0.5% of that bankroll per trade, same stops, leverage caps, and halts). These are scale twins, not new strategy trials. They do not increase N. The DSR trial count stays 434.

Retired and halted books have no twin. Existing books, bankrolls, latches, and history are unchanged.
